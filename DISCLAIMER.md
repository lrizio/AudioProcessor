## Sound levels and hearing

The program can raise levels (gain, AGC, compressor make-up) by a large amount, and a wrong setting, a
feedback loop or a sudden loud signal can be very loud. Start with the volume low, use the Mute button
while you set up, and be careful with headphones. Listening at high volume can permanently damage your
hearing.

## Connecting it to a radio

Audio Processor is intended for **receive** audio. If you route its output into a radio's transmit audio
input (for example through a virtual audio cable or a radio's USB audio codec), you can cause the radio to
transmit, or transmit something you did not intend. Check what each device is connected to before you
start the audio. You are solely responsible for what is transmitted and for operating within the
conditions of your own licence and the regulations of your country. The program never keys a radio and has
no transmit control.

## Recording
